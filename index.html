Student research project

Growing more nutritious mung bean sprouts.

We're testing whether adding magnesium, zinc and protein to the water mung beans sprout in can raise the nutritional value of the sprouts themselves.

See our method Meet the team

About the project

A cheap, everyday food with room to grow.

Mung bean sprouts are inexpensive, fast to grow and eaten widely across Asia. Because a sprout draws everything it needs from the water around it during germination, the growing solution is a simple place to intervene.

Sproutforce Alliance is investigating how effective it is to enrich that water with specific nutrients, and whether the sprouts take those nutrients up in a way that meaningfully increases their nutritional value.

Research question

How does adding magnesium, zinc or protein to the water solution used to grow mung bean sprouts affect the nutritional value of the sprouts?

What we're adding

Three nutrients, tested one at a time.

Mg

Magnesium

A mineral involved in hundreds of enzyme reactions in the body, and at the centre of the chlorophyll molecule in plants.

Zn

Zinc

A trace mineral important for immune function and growth, and a common deficiency in diets that rely heavily on staple crops.

Pr

Protein

The macronutrient that builds and repairs tissue. We're testing whether protein in the growing water changes the protein content of the sprouts.

How we're testing it

Our approach.

  1. 01

    Prepare the solutions

    Each batch of mung beans is grown in water enriched with a single nutrient — magnesium, zinc or protein — alongside a control batch grown in plain water.

  2. 02

    Grow the sprouts

    All batches are sprouted under the same conditions so the growing solution is the only thing that differs between them.

  3. 03

    Measure and compare

    We measure the nutrient content of each batch and compare it against the control to see whether, and by how much, enrichment made a difference.

  4. 04

    Write it up

    Our findings from the single-nutrient tests are going into a research paper, which we're drafting now.

Where we are

Project roadmap.

In progress

Phase 1 · Single-nutrient testing

Testing magnesium, zinc and protein individually to see how each one affects the sprouts on its own.

In progress

Research paper

Drafting a paper that documents our method and the results of the individual nutrient tests.

Planned

Phase 2 · Multi-nutrient testing

Combining nutrients in the same growing solution to see how they interact, and whether a combination works better than any one alone.
